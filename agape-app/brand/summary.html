Games, concerts, college sports, festivals and arenas of every size. Agape is built for any live event, and our first venues are likely to be in Kentucky.

How Agape earns

  • A share of every sale made through Agape
  • A delivery fee on every order brought to a seat
  • More with every level: sponsors and deals in the moment, play along, tickets

Fans pay Agape; Agape keeps its share and pays the stands and runners. Nobody is ever collected from.

Why Agape wins

  • Times that come true. Every time shown is when it reaches the seat, measured from how fast each kitchen and runner is going right now.
  • Delivery that pays. Orders timed to be ready together and carried several to a route: built around deliveries per runner hour.
  • One set of books. Fans, stands, runners, the venue and Agape see one ledger, balanced to the cent.
  • A venue in an afternoon. Live by filling in a form. No hardware, no integration project.

Where it goes

Food is the way in. A fan's level, points and history follow them to every venue on Agape: deals and sponsors in the moment, play along, tickets, and a community that lasts past the final whistle. Nearby businesses Agape sends fans to can become its card-processing customers.

Built, not slides

  • Six apps on one system: fans, runners, stands, the venue, partners, Agape's team
  • Ready for a first venue: real nights, a live console, reports that reconcile at close
  • Payments ready to connect: cards held at order, charged on delivery
